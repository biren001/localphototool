You are offline — that's fine

There is no internet connection right now, but the compressor runs entirely on your device. Anything already cached will keep working.

Open the compressor
  • Compressing JPG, PNG and WebP works offline once the page has been opened before.
  • AVIF encoding needs the AOM codec, which is fetched once and then cached — open the tool once online to have it available offline too.
  • Nothing you do offline leaves your device, exactly like when you are online.
